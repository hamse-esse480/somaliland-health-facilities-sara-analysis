# Somaliland Health Facilities SARA Survey 2022

## Project Overview

This project presents an Excel-based analysis of the Somaliland Health Facilities Service Availability and Readiness Assessment (SARA) Survey 2022.

The analysis focuses on understanding the availability and readiness of health facilities across Somaliland and identifying key patterns in the survey data.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel

## Dataset

- Somaliland Health Facilities SARA Survey 2022
- 348 health facilities
- 6 regions
- District coverage summarized below

## Analysis

The project includes data cleaning, validation, descriptive analysis, and summary of key findings.

## Key Findings

- **Health facilities:** The dataset included 348 health facilities across 6 regions and 28 districts.
- **Regional distribution:** Marodi Jeex had the largest number of facilities (82), followed by Sanaag (65), Sool (64), Togdheer (52), Awdal (51), and Saaxil (34).
- **Facility type:** Health Centers were the largest facility category (185), followed by Health Posts (129), Hospitals (32), and District Hospitals (2).
- **Maternal care services:** 84 facilities were recorded as providing maternal care services, while 264 records had missing information.
- **TB treatment services:** 13 facilities were recorded as providing TB treatment services, while 335 records had missing information.
- **Ownership:** 132 facilities were recorded as MOHD-owned, while 216 records had missing ownership information.
- **Priority facilities:** 132 facilities were identified as priority facilities.
- **Priority needs:** Among the recorded priority facilities, 98 required medical equipment, sterilization, vaccine refrigeration, and lighting, while 34 required lighting.
- **Location data:** Location information was missing for 216 of the 348 facilities.
- **Population served:** Among the 38 facilities with available population-served data, the mean was approximately 13,665 people, with a median of 12,692 and a range of 1,513–29,000.

### Overall Finding

The dataset covered 348 health facilities across 6 regions and 28 districts, with Health Centers representing the largest facility type. However, substantial missing information was observed for several variables, particularly location, ownership, maternal care services, TB treatment services, and population served. These data limitations should be considered when interpreting the findings.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate practical skills in health data management, data cleaning, descriptive analysis, and visualization using Microsoft Excel.
